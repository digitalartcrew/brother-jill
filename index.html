The Problem

The system was
never built for them.

Young Black men face compounding systems of trauma, neglect, and criminalization. Without culturally grounded intervention, the cycle continues.

⚠️

Systemic Criminalization

Schools and institutions often punish rather than support Black youth, pushing them out instead of lifting them up.

💔

Unaddressed Trauma

High exposure to community violence, loss, and systemic racism creates deep emotional wounds that go untreated.

🚫

Cultural Disconnection

The absence of programs rooted in lived Black experience leaves youth without the mirrors they need to see themselves fully.

Our Mission

We build men,
not just moments.

"You are not behind — you are becoming."

To guide young Black boys and men from underserved communities toward healing, self-discovery, and purpose-driven living through culturally grounded mentorship and mindset development. We envision a world where healing is normalized, purpose is pursued, and legacy is built through self-awareness and accountability.

About the Founder

Brother Jill

Cultural Educator • Movement Builder • Founder

Brother Jill is a cultural educator and movement builder rooted in truth, healing, and transformation. From lived experience to leadership, he built the Big Brother Jill Foundation to bridge wisdom, wellness, and worth for a new generation of young kings.

His work is not theory — it is lived. Every program he creates, every circle he facilitates, and every conversation he holds is shaped by the understanding that healing must come before achievement, and that identity is the foundation of everything.
