# CLAUDE.md

## Organization: *[Your Nonprofit Name]*

## Mission

To guide young Black boys and men from underserved communities toward healing, self-discovery, and purpose-driven living through culturally grounded mentorship and mindset development.

---

## 1. Core Philosophy

### Guiding Beliefs

* Healing is foundational before achievement
* Identity shapes outcomes
* Representation and cultural grounding are essential
* Brotherhood builds accountability and belonging
* Exposure creates possibility

### Core Pillars

1. **Healing** – Emotional intelligence, trauma awareness, mental wellness
2. **Identity** – Cultural pride, self-worth, personal narrative
3. **Purpose** – Goal-setting, career pathways, life vision
4. **Brotherhood** – Community, mentorship, accountability
5. **Execution** – Discipline, consistency, real-world skills

---

## 2. Target Audience

### Primary

* Black boys and young men (ages 8–24)
* Located in underserved or under-resourced communities

### Secondary

* Parents/guardians
* Schools and educators
* Community organizations
* Corporate sponsors & donors

---

## 3. Programs & Offerings

### 1. Mentorship Program

* 1:1 and group mentorship
* Weekly check-ins
* Monthly goal tracking

### 2. Healing Circles

* Safe spaces for emotional expression
* Facilitated discussions on trauma, identity, and growth

### 3. Mindset & Leadership Development

* Confidence building
* Communication skills
* Decision-making frameworks

### 4. Exposure Experiences

* College visits
* Career days
* Entrepreneurship workshops

### 5. Life Skills Training

* Financial literacy
* Conflict resolution
* Health & wellness

---

## 4. Brand Voice & Messaging

### Tone

* Empowering, authentic, culturally aware
* Direct but compassionate
* Inspirational without being cliché

### Key Messaging Themes

* “You are not behind—you are becoming.”
* “Healing is strength.”
* “Your story matters.”
* “Purpose over pressure.”
* “We build men, not just moments.”

### Do:

* Speak with cultural awareness and authenticity
* Highlight real stories and transformations
* Use clear, simple language

### Don’t:

* Sound overly academic or robotic
* Use savior narratives
* Generalize or stereotype

---

## 5. AI Content Guidelines (For Claude / ChatGPT Use)

### When Generating Content:

* Prioritize **impact over volume**
* Make content **emotionally resonant and actionable**
* Always align with **mission and pillars**

### Content Types to Generate:

* Social media posts (Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn)
* Grant proposals
* Program outlines
* Email campaigns
* Website copy
* Event scripts

### Style Requirements:

* Hook-driven openings
* Short, powerful sentences
* Clear calls to action
* Use storytelling when possible
